CONTENT GOVERNANCE

How we publish case studies.

Every case study and product story carries a publication status, so approval and confidentiality are always clear. These are content-governance states, not claims about any specific product's capability.

Public

Approved for external publication with all necessary permissions.

Approval Pending

Awaiting business, client or confidentiality approval before publishing.

Internal Only

Documented for internal reference; not published externally.

Client Restricted

Details restricted by client confidentiality requirements.
